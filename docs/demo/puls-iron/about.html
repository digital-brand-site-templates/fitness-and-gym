Two coaches, one rule, twelve years

Puls-iron is a coached gym. Everything about the place — the member cap, the re-assessments, the coaches on the floor — follows from that.

Our story

From six racks to two floors

  1. 2014

    400 square metres and a rule

    Dana Kowalczyk and Reza Farahani open Puls-iron in a former print works with six racks, 90 members and the promise that every one of them gets a written programme.

  2. 2017

    The twelve-week re-assessment

    Retention was the problem every gym had. We started measuring every member again every twelve weeks and rewriting the plan. Year-one renewals went from 61% to 84%.

  3. 2020

    24/7 access, coaches till ten

    Fob access around the clock for shift workers and parents, with two coaches on the strength floor from 6am to 10pm every day.

  4. 2023

    The second floor and the cap

    We took the floor above: a dedicated mobility studio, a sled track and eight more racks. Then we capped membership at 1,400 so it would never feel full.

  5. Today

    18 coaches, 60+ classes, 92% renewals

    Still two founders on the floor most mornings. Still nobody trains here without a plan.

How onboarding works

Your first three weeks at Puls-iron

01

Week one: the movement screen

A 45-minute session with a coach. We look at how you squat, hinge, push, pull and carry, ask about injuries and your week, and write a four-week plan you can actually keep.

  • Joint-by-joint mobility check
  • Baseline strength and conditioning numbers
  • A plan built around your available days
Book a movement screen
02

Week two: coached sessions

Your first three programmed sessions are shadowed by a coach. Not a personal-training upsell — it is how every member learns the floor and the lifts.

  • Technique cues on every main lift
  • Introduction to two classes that fit your goal
See the classes
03

Week three onward: train, then re-measure

Train on your own or in classes. Every twelve weeks we measure again and rewrite the plan. That cadence is why people are still here in year three.
